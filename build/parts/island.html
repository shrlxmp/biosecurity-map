The biosecurity field, drawn as one island

Every organisation working on biosecurity, placed on a single landmass. The coastline is not decoration: it is drawn around where the organisations actually are, so a crowded part of the field becomes a wide province and a neglected one becomes a narrow spit of land.

The island reads west to east, in the order a threat is met. It makes landfall on the Prevention shore, crosses the Detection narrows, and reaches the Response interior. The southern plain is the Foundation — the funders, teachers and coordinators everything else stands on.
